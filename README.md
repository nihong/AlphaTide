# AlphaTide 🌊

**AlphaTide V6.0: The VCP Momentum Sniper** (游资刺客降维打击量化系统)

AlphaTide is an institutional-grade, fully autonomous quantitative investment framework designed for A-shares, HK-shares, and Global Macro ETFs. Version 6.0 completely abandons mechanical "volume spike" buying and introduces top-tier Wall Street proprietary trading mechanics: **RPS Momentum Ranking, Volatility Contraction Pattern (VCP), and strict Macro Weather filters.**

## The Alpha Philosophy (V6.0 Paradigm Shift)

We believe that true alpha comes from buying the strongest assets at their quietest moments, under the best macro weather. 
True alpha is generated by:
1. **RPS Momentum Filtering**: Only trading the Top 10% strongest stocks in the entire market (Relative Price Strength > 90). The strong get stronger.
2. **VCP Quant Radar**: Scanning for Volatility Contraction. We look for stocks where price action becomes deadly quiet and volume dries up completely, followed by a sudden breakout.
3. **Macro Weather Lock**: Executing a mandatory pause on all LONG trading when the broader market (CSI 300) falls below its 60-day moving average.
4. **Absolute Defense**: Tight 1.5x ATR trailing stops to ruthlessly cut losses while letting the VCP breakouts run.

## V6.0 Architecture

AlphaTide operates via 3 core autonomous modules:

1. **🛡️ Universe Screener (`src/universe_screener.py`)**: The Bouncer. Cleanses ST stocks and calculates RPS across the entire market, filtering down to only the Top 10% strongest momentum leaders.
2. **📡 Quant Radar (`src/quant_radar.py`)**: The Detective. Scans the RPS leaders for 15-day Volatility Contraction Patterns (VCP) and volume dry-ups.
3. **⚔️ Execution Engine (`src/execution_engine.py`)**: The Sniper. Reads the Macro Weather (CSI 300 Trend), executes concentrated trades (Max 2 positions), and rigidly enforces risk management (ATR trailing stops).
